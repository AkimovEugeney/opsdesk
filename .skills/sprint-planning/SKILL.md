# Sprint planning

Use at the start of a weekly iteration after closing or reviewing the previous one.

1. Review unfinished work and its recorded reason; do not silently carry it over.
2. Confirm the learner's available capacity for this week.
3. Define one observable business Sprint Goal and a concise learning goal visible to the learner.
4. Offer realistic candidates only after the prerequisite check in `docs/learning/task-manager.md`. Use concrete concept evidence and the recall queue in `docs/learning/skills-matrix.md`; introduce at most one major new concept per ticket. Do not fill the plan with repeats of parked topics.
5. Let the learner refine and estimate the candidates.
6. Record dependencies, risks and unknowns already discovered.
7. Check that the agreed scope fits capacity, including explanation, manual practice, short recall, tests, review and debugging; split oversized work. A blocked feature does not force its entire unfamiliar prerequisite chain into this sprint.
8. Persist the plan in `work/sprints/` and update `current-state.md`.

Use hours, not story points. Do not require a ticket count, daily stand-ups or padded hours.
