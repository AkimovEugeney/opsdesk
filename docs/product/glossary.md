# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Customer request | A request raised by a customer that requires a response or action. |
| Internal incident | An internally reported event that disrupts or risks disrupting operations. |
| Operator | A person who handles customer requests. |
| Incident responder | A person coordinating investigation and mitigation of an internal incident. |
| Owner | The currently responsible person or team for an item of work. |
| Update | A recorded communication about progress or outcome. |
| Resolution | The recorded outcome that completes an item. |
