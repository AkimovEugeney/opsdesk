# Product rules

1. Customer requests and internal incidents are distinct business concepts, even where their workflows look similar.
2. Each active item has a visible current owner.
3. Material progress and outcomes are recorded as updates.
4. Customers see only information intended for them; internal incident details remain internal unless explicitly designated otherwise.
5. A closed item retains its recorded outcome for later review.

This document is the product source of truth beneath ticket-specific clarifications. It intentionally does not specify APIs, storage, technical architecture or implementation.
