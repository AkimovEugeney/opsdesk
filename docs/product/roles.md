# Product roles

## Customer

Creates and follows their own requests and receives updates relevant to them.

## Operator

Receives, clarifies, classifies and progresses customer requests within their responsibility.

## Incident responder

Coordinates investigation and mitigation of internal incidents and records operational updates.

## Team lead

Oversees work distribution, service quality and process outcomes.

## Administrator

Maintains permitted access and reference information under approved product rules.

Specific permissions and assignment boundaries are defined by future tickets, not by this bootstrap document.
