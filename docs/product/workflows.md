# Product workflows

## Customer request

1. A customer submits a request.
2. An operator reviews and clarifies it when needed.
3. The request is worked on by the responsible team.
4. The customer receives relevant progress updates.
5. The request reaches an outcome and is closed.

## Internal incident

1. A team member registers an operational incident.
2. An incident responder assesses and coordinates the response.
3. The team records material progress and mitigation.
4. The incident receives a documented outcome.

Exact statuses, priorities, escalation conditions and service targets remain ticket-level product decisions.
