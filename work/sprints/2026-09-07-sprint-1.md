## Retrospective

- What went well? Three small read-focused slices were delivered with real HTTP evidence and same-session review; the learner wrote the key code and the observed behavior matched the agreed acceptance criteria; process friction was corrected quickly when the learner raised it.
- What slowed us down? (1) The window ended 2026-09-13 but the iteration stayed `Active` until 2026-09-22, so review, retro and the capacity update were skipped and no work was planned for 2026-09-14 — 2026-09-20. (2) The planned total (0.67–1.0 h) was far below the confirmed 3–6 h capacity and only 0.75 h was recorded — the unused-capacity cause is still not recorded, repeating Sprint 0's gap. (3) OPS-002's actual time was never recorded, so one of three tickets cannot be compared at all. (4) Two sessions went to learning-system rule changes: real work, but unplanned and unattributed to any ticket, which diluted the Goal. (5) The Planned tickets table never received the agreed OPS-005 row; it was reconstructed from the progress table at closure.
- Where did estimates miss, and why? Both estimated tickets overran by about 50% (0.17 → 0.25 h; 0.33 → 0.50 h). In absolute terms the misses are small, and both actuals include teaching before implementation plus manual validation and review. Pattern: the estimate covers implementation, while the learning and verification dialogue around it is estimated as zero.
- What engineering/process lesson follows? Estimate-versus-actual evidence only exists if the closure work happens inside the window; an iteration left open loses its own review data, and a ticket actual that is not recorded at the moment of delivery is lost for good (OPS-002).
- What was retained and independently applied after a delay? `http-input-validation` got its first verified independent application in OPS-004 on 2026-09-08 (ParseIntPipe, range check, propagation through the layers) — the changed-condition repeat of OPS-000/OPS-002 evidence. SQL text matching from OPS-005 is pending its explanation. The internal recall opportunities dated 2026-09-09 (`raw-result-contract`, `nullish-defaults`), 2026-09-11 (`sql-select-filter`) and 2026-09-15 (`sql-left-join`) were not exercised because no product work ran in that period; they remain internal opportunities and are not prerequisites for the next task.
- What one or two concrete changes will we make next sprint? (1) Close each iteration inside its own window: the last session of the week performs review, retro and the capacity update even when tickets are unfinished, and a ticket's actual time is written when it is delivered. (2) State estimates as implementation plus a learning/validation allowance (for example "20 min implementation + 10 min teaching and review"), so the recorded actual can be compared against a realistic number.
