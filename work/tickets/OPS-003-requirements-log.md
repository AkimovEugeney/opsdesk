| 2026-09-26 | Who may assign, and who may become owner? | An authenticated current user with role `OPERATOR` and the `canManageAppeals` permission may assign. This is the working Product Owner decision because no separate business source specifies otherwise; revise it if business later gives a contrary rule. | Agreed product decision / documented assumption |
| 2026-09-26 | What state transition is allowed? | `NEW` → `IN_PROGRESS` with the selected owner; `IN_PROGRESS` → `IN_PROGRESS` with a possibly changed owner. `CLOSED` is immutable. Every successful assignment changes `updatedAt`; owner and status must persist together. | Product Owner decision |
| 2026-09-26 | What errors are part of the contract? | Missing session: existing `401`. Authenticated user without `canManageAppeals`: `403` before revealing resource state. Missing appeal: `404`. Missing selected assignee: `404`. A selected existing `CLIENT`, malformed/non-integer ID, or non-positive ID: `400`. Closed appeal, or an otherwise eligible appeal that becomes closed before the write commits: `409`. | Agreed product decision |
| 2026-09-26 | What integrity property must implementation preserve? | A successful response must never leave a `NEW` appeal with an owner, nor mutate a `CLOSED` appeal. Authorization and the eligible-owner check must be based on server-side records, not client-supplied actor data. The write must protect the status condition at persistence time, because a concurrent close may happen after an earlier read. | Engineering constraint |
