## Business value

Every protected action in OpsDesk depends on knowing who the caller is and what role they have. Today that decision is made separately in each endpoint. The next product slice (OPS-015, customers see their own appeals) adds more endpoints whose correctness depends entirely on it. A wrong or forgotten check there exposes one customer's data to another.

## Context

- Choice of order made by the learner on 2026-10-20: prepare the caller identification before the customer-facing read slice.
- Protected today: `GET /auth/me`, `POST /appeals`, `PATCH /appeals/:id/owner`, `PATCH /appeals/:id/close`. Public today: login, logout (idempotent, always `204`), `GET /appeals`, `GET /appeals/:id`.
- The learner proposed guards as the mechanism. That is the learner's proposal to be assessed in Design Review, not an accepted constraint.

## Visible requirements

- Callers are identified the same way on every protected endpoint: same result for a missing, unknown, expired or ended session.
- The identified user and role are available to the endpoint without re-deriving them.
- Each endpoint's access intent is explicit: protected or deliberately public. A newly added endpoint must not become protected or public by accident.
- Existing protected endpoints preserve status codes, error bodies and refusal order, subject to the guard-before-validation decision tracked in the requirements log (for example, a client is refused with `403` before the appeal is looked up on close).
- Scope change accepted on 2026-10-21: `GET /appeals` and `GET /appeals/:id` require a valid session in this ticket. Caller-specific visibility remains OPS-015; authentication alone does not restrict a customer to their own appeals.
- Login and logout remain public. Logout stays idempotent, including with no cookie or an invalid session.

## Acceptance criteria

- For every protected endpoint, including both appeal GET routes after this change: missing, unknown, expired or ended session → `401`; valid session → the previous response, subject to existing role and permission rules.
- Login and logout do not require a valid session. Login still validates credentials.
- Role and permission refusals (`403`) and all other existing results stay as they were.
- Logout works as before: with cookie, without cookie, repeated.
- Build and existing tests pass; each criterion is confirmed by a real request.
- Code Review has no open BLOCKER.
