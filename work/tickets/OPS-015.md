# OPS-015 Customers see their own appeals

- Status: Draft — follows OPS-014; open decisions listed below are not yet agreed
- Estimate: not recorded
- Iteration: week of 2026-10-19 (planned after OPS-014)

Draft product contract (Codex as Product Owner, 2026-10-20; may be challenged):

- A signed-in customer sees all their own appeals (active and closed), newest first: title, status, creation date, outcome if closed. Their card adds description, closing date and the assigned operator's name if any.
- A customer never sees others' appeals; a foreign appeal is indistinguishable from a missing one. No sign-in, no appeal data.
- After sign-in an operator sees the active queue as before and can open any appeal, including closed ones. `CAN_MANAGE_APPEALS` does not affect reading.
- A closed appeal without an outcome shows an empty outcome. Empty list is a normal success. No admin role. Backend API only; no pagination or filters; URLs unchanged.

Open for the learner's decision: whether anonymous read is closed fully (recommended) or the queue stays public without customer names; whether automated access tests are part of this ticket.

Access boundary: Design Review before implementation and independent review after.
